Bricks Mobile Application Privacy Policy

Application: Bricks Operated by: Brick By Brick Enterprises Effective Date: October 16, 2024 Status: Active
Summary of Core Principles: At Bricks App, your trust is paramount. We do not sell your personal data. We only collect the information necessary to provide site progress tracking, construction workflows, budget calculations, and secure enterprise communications.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bricks ("the Application"), developed, distributed, and maintained by Brick By Brick Enterprises ("Company", "we", "our", or "us"). We specialize in construction management, project monitoring, materials workflow, and client collaboration.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal, professional, and project data is collected, processed, and safeguarded when you access or interact with the Bricks Mobile App or its backend web services (accessible via https://bricks-backend-fk3q.onrender.com/api).

2. Information We Collect

To deliver our services effectively, we collect the following categories of information:

A. User & Account Credentials

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, contact telephone/mobile number, email address, and office/residential address.
  • Authentication Secrets: User-created Secure Personal Identification Numbers (PIN) and encrypted login tokens.
  • Profile Image: Avatar or photograph chosen and uploaded to represent you in the workspace.

B. Construction Site & Operational Records

  • Site Progress & Logs: Real-time work descriptions, daily updates, construction milestones, and supervisor notes.
  • Financials & Invoicing: Budget figures, line-item expenses, invoices, and payment summaries.
  • Materials & Vendors: Material requisitions, supplier records, and labour database entries entered in the Human Resources panel.

C. Media & Photographic Files

  • Camera Captures & Gallery Photos: Site images, receipts, material delivery slips, and progress photos uploaded directly through the application.

D. Technical & Device Data

  • Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, network connection status, and app crash logs.
  • Local Cache: Secure storage of session JWT tokens via SharedPreferences to keep you logged in securely.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The Application requests explicit runtime permissions on your mobile operating system. These permissions are strictly mapped to features as follows:

Permission System Identifier Explicit Purpose
Camera CAMERA Allows users to photograph ongoing site works, material deliveries, receipts, and profile portraits directly within the app.
Storage & Photos READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Enables uploading site records and profile images from your device photo library, and storing generated PDF invoices locally.
Internet INTERNET Connects to our secure cloud backend server for synchronizing project data, downloading invoices, and authenticating users.

4. How We Use Information

The collected information is utilized solely for:

  • Authenticating your identity and preventing unauthorized access.
  • Facilitating site monitoring, daily logs, and stakeholder updates.
  • Generating downloadable and printable PDF invoices and budget reports.
  • Syncing images and material requests with cloud storage.
  • Communicating vital technical support updates, notifications, and security notices.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ robust technical and organizational security mechanisms. All communications between the mobile application and cloud servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Authentication is governed by signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT).

Operational data is housed within secure cloud data centers with continuous monitoring. We retain user and project data only as long as necessary to support project continuity, warranty documentation, and legal compliance.

6. Sharing of Information

Strict Policy: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information or site data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

Data is shared only in the following limited situations:

  • Authorized Project Collaborators: Users granted access to your specific site project (e.g., project managers, assigned clients).
  • Infrastructure Partners: Secure cloud hosting, database, and backend processing providers bound by non-disclosure agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When necessary to comply with valid legal processes, law enforcement mandates, or to protect the safety of users and public welfare.

7. User Control & Data Deletion Rights

Users hold full rights over their personal information. You can edit your profile information at any time directly through the Settings > Account User Profile section of the app.

Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You may request the permanent deletion of your account, profile, and personal records by emailing our Data Protection Desk at support@brickbybrick.enterprises. Verification will be performed and eligible data removed within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bricks application is strictly designed for enterprise, professional, and commercial construction operations. It is not intended for use by minors under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal details from children.

9. Policy Revisions

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be indicated by a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the Application signifies your consent to the amended policy terms.
