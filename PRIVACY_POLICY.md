## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide
When you register, log in, or update your profile in the Application, we may collect:
- **Contact Details:** Full name, mobile telephone number, email address, physical address.
- **Account Credentials:** Secure PIN or password used to authenticate your account.
- **Profile Image:** Photographs or avatars you choose to upload as your account profile picture.
- **Human Resources & Directory Data:** Names, roles, vendor details, and contact numbers of site personnel, labour, and contractors you enter into the HR & Vendor directory.

### B. Project & Site Operational Data
To provide construction management capabilities, the Application collects:
- **Project Information:** Site names, project timelines, location markers, and progress milestones.
- **Site Updates & Logs:** Daily progress logs, notes, work descriptions, and checklists.
- **Financial & Budget Records:** Budget allocations, expense tracking entries, material costs, invoice records, and billing data.
- **Material Management Data:** Inventory items, quantities, dispatch receipts, and supply requirements.

### C. Media & Device Files
- **Photos & Images:** Images captured via your device camera or selected from your photo gallery/storage for site progress documentation, damage reports, delivery slips, and profile identification.
- **Documents & PDFs:** Invoices and site reports generated or printed within the Application.

### D. Automatically Collected Device & Technical Information
- **Device Details:** Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, unique device identifiers.
- **Network & Diagnostics:** IP address, internet connection status, application crash reports, and performance metrics.
- **Local Storage:** Authentication tokens (JWT) and cached user preference settings saved securely using device storage (`SharedPreferences`).

---

## 3. Device Permissions Requested

The Application requests the following permissions on your mobile device for specific functionalities:
1. **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Required to allow you to take real-time photos of site progress, construction activities, material slips, and profile photos directly from the app.
2. **Storage / Photo Library (`android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** Required to allow you to select existing photos or documents from your device gallery to upload to the server.
3. **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to connect to our secure cloud API server (`bricks-backend-fk3q.onrender.com`) to synchronize projects, updates, budgets, and user accounts.

You can modify or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings, though certain features (such as image uploading) will not function without them.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We process and utilize your information for the following legitimate business purposes:
- **Account Administration:** To authenticate users, verify phone numbers and secure PINs, and maintain session security.
- **Construction Site Management:** To enable collaboration, record daily site work, track materials, and monitor ongoing project timelines.
- **Financial & Budgeting Tools:** To compute budget allocations, log expenses, and generate professional PDF invoices and reports.
- **File Uploads & Cloud Sync:** To securely store site photographs and documents on cloud infrastructure for real-time visibility across stakeholders.
- **Customer Support & Communications:** To assist with technical issues, system updates, critical alerts, and account inquiries.
- **Security & Integrity:** To prevent unauthorized access, detect fraudulent activities, and maintain operational stability.

## 5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

- **Security Measures:** We implement industry-standard security practices, including Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (SSL/TLS) for data in transit, encrypted storage, and JSON Web Token (JWT) authorization protocols.
- **Cloud Hosting:** Operational data and media are stored on secure cloud backend servers (hosted with Render and MongoDB database clusters).
- **Data Retention:** We retain your personal data and project history for as long as your account is active or as necessary to fulfill construction project tracking, warranty compliance, tax, or legal requirements.
- **Local Device Cache:** Session tokens and cached preferences are stored locally in private application sandbox storage and are cleared upon signing out.
